# **The Foundational Infrastructure for the Open Agentic Web**

### The coming AI agent economy will be a battle between closed "walled gardens" and an open "web of agents." Our mission is to build the open-source, on-chain foundation that ensures the open web wins.

---

## **Overview**

Prometheus Protocol is the complete economic engine for the decentralized AI economy. It provides a unified solution for the three pillars of a trustworthy open ecosystem:

1.  **Verifiable Trust:** A decentralized "app store" and software supply chain that replaces corporate gatekeepers with cryptographic proof and community-driven audits.
2.  **Secure Identity:** A universal passport for humans and agents, built on open standards like OAuth 2.1, enabling secure interaction across the entire open web.
3.  **Direct Payments:** The native payment rail for the M2M economy, allowing any service to be monetized with low-friction, on-chain micro-transactions.

By combining these layers, Prometheus provides the essential infrastructure for a new generation of decentralized applications and a fair, competitive agentic economy.

---

## **The Roadmap**

Our journey is structured in ambitious phases, building from a solid foundation towards a vibrant, trusted, and self-sustaining ecosystem.

### **Phase 0: The Foundation - "Project Hephaestus" (✅ COMPLETE)**

**Goal:** Forge the complete, end-to-end stack for secure identity and payments.
-   **Outcome:** A production-ready OAuth 2.1 provider and a full suite of SDKs, enabling any developer to build and monetize secure, on-chain services.

---

### **Phase 1: The Trust Layer - "Project Arsenal" (✅ COMPLETE)**

**Goal:** Build the premier, high-trust software supply chain for provably safe services.
-   **Outcome:** A feature-complete, on-chain App Store and Trust Hub, including a service registry, an audit and bounty marketplace, and a full command-line interface for developers and auditors.

---

### **Phase 2: Ecosystem Growth - "Project Alliance" (✅ COMPLETE)**

**Goal:** Build the complete economic and social infrastructure to bootstrap a self-sustaining economy via the **Alpha Flywheel Initiative**.
-   **The Supply Engine (Developer Incentives):**
    -   [x] **Service Bounty System:** Launched a public bounty board where developers can claim **USDC rewards** for building and certifying high-value services.
    -   [x] **Genesis Grant Program:** Deployed an MVP process for funding larger, community-proposed projects with USDC grants.
-   **The Trust Engine (Auditor Incentives):**
    -   [x] **Auditor Hub & Reputation System:** Deployed a transparent marketplace where vetted auditors can stake USDC, claim audit bounties, and build their on-chain reputation.
    -   [x] **Real-Time Notifications:** Integrated a Discord bot to instantly alert the community when new services are submitted for audit.
-   **The Demand Engine (End-User Incentives):**
    -   [x] **Usage Mining System:** Launched a client-agnostic system for developers to integrate into their services, allowing them to reward end-users with **USDC** for their activity.
    -   [x] **Multi-Platform Showcase:** Built and demonstrated AI agents using services from the App Store across multiple platforms, including **n8n, PortalOne, and VSCode**.
-   **The Foundation (Documentation & Comms):**
    -   [x] **Developer Documentation Hub:** Launched a comprehensive documentation site with quickstarts, tutorials, and core concepts.
    -   [x] **Community & Marketing Launch:** Executed a full marketing strategy, including launching a new "On-chain AI" tech meetup.

---

### **Phase 3: The Autonomous Economy - "Project Agora" (⏳ IN PROGRESS)**

**Goal:** Evolve from a team-led project into a self-sustaining, public utility governed by its own economic mechanics and community stewardship.
-   **Protocol Sustainability:**
    -   [ ] **The Handover:** Transition control of key protocol parameters (e.g., managing the list of vetted auditors, fee structures) to a transparent, on-chain process, cementing its status as a decentralized public good.
    -   [ ] **Treasury & Fee Switch:** Implement a protocol fee switch, allowing a micro-fee on transactions to be directed to a community-managed treasury to fund ongoing development and security.
-   **Advanced Economic Primitives:**
    -   [ ] **Security Bonds:** Enable developers to stake **USDC** as a security bond, providing an economic guarantee of their service's integrity.
    -   [ ] **Atomic Revenue Sharing:** Enhance the SDK to allow services to programmatically split incoming revenue with other services, enabling more complex and collaborative applications.

---

### **Phase 4: Mainstream Adoption - "Project Pantheon" (🚀 UP NEXT)**

**Goal:** Become the ubiquitous, de facto standard for trust and commerce in the decentralized AI ecosystem.
-   **Strategic Partnerships:**
    -   [ ] Forge partnerships with major AI agent platforms and developer ecosystems to integrate Prometheus as their default trust and monetization layer.
-   **Protocol Standardization:**
    -   [ ] Formalize the Prometheus verification and identity standards into a public ICRC proposal for the entire Internet Computer ecosystem.
-   **Economic Scale:**
    -   [ ] Achieve a major economic milestone, such as processing over $1M in total transaction volume through the protocol's payment layer.
